My Family

There are four members in my immediate family: my father, my mother, my younger sister, and me. I also have a larger family of 16 members, including my grandparents, uncles, aunts, and cousins. Although we do not all live together, we often meet during festivals, birthdays, and other special occasions. I really enjoy spending time with my extended family, especially my cousins, because we get to laugh, celebrate, share memories, and enjoy each other’s company.
