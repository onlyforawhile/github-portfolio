My Favorite Time of the Year

My favorite time of the year is during Dashain and Tihar. During these festivals, the atmosphere in Nepal, especially Kathmandu, feels more lively and joyful. I especially enjoy celebrating Dashain with my family and cousins, where we get to spend time together, laugh, eat delicious food, and enjoy the celebrations. I also enjoy Tihar, especially making rangolis and seeing everything decorated and bright. What I love most about this time of the year is the happiness, togetherness, and special feeling that comes with celebrating with family.
