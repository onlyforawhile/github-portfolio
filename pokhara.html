My Favorite Place I Have Visited

Among the places I have visited, Pokhara is my favorite. I have not travelled to many places yet, but my visit to Pokhara was one of my most enjoyable experiences. I visited during the New Year, when the city was filled with celebrations, street stalls, crowds, and a lively atmosphere. There were many different foods and things to explore, and I also got to see visitors from different countries. The beautiful views of the Himalayas made the experience even more memorable, and I enjoyed spending that time with my family.
