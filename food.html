My Favorite Food

I enjoy many different kinds of food, but my favorite is white sauce pasta, especially Alfredo pasta. I like its creamy taste and find it really comforting. I also enjoy Nepali foods such as sukuti and sekuwa, as well as foods like pizza. When it comes to desserts, chocolate cake, pastries, and chocolate are some of my favorites. I enjoy trying different foods and discovering new flavors.
